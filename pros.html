What's in every lead

Each lead is a real submission from our funnel, not a scraped list. Here is what you get with every one:

  • Homeowner name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • ZIP code
  • Trade and job type
  • Job size (pool gallons, home sq ft, or electric bill)
  • Budget range they selected
  • Own vs. rent
  • Timestamp and consent record
